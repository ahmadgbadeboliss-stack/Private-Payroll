## Tweet 2 — Technical insight

> The core primitive: a ZK proof of correct sum over hidden values.
>
> Each payout is sealed as H(recipient, amount, salt) inside a Merkle tree.
> The circuit proves Σ(hidden amounts) = declaredTotal, every leaf is in the
> published root, every recipient is authorized, and the pool covers it all.
>
> Auditable to the cent. Readable by no one. `disclose()` only where we
> chose to.
